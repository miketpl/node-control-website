Thanks for using the Node Control Free Edition.

Version 0.9.46 is out. If you are still on 0.9.44 or 0.9.45, please update, because those versions will not show the new 14-day Pro trial.

On 0.9.46 you can start the trial from Help → Start trial… whenever you are ready. It gives you 14 days with the full Engineer tab, all reports, your own AI provider, and no device limit. After 14 days the app goes back to the Free Edition you have now: L2 Topology, Config Backup, Save Config, Find Device and Inventory, on up to 25 devices. Monitor, Discover, IPAM, the Firewall Analyser and the Meraki Configurator are not in the trial. It is one trial per email address, and one per computer.

To update, open Node Control and use Help, then Check for Updates, or let it run the check it already does at launch. Install 0.9.46 over the copy you have now. Your library, settings and credentials are kept. If the in-app update does not start, go to https://nodecontrol.io/#download, enter the download code you already have, and download the installer for your computer. The steps are also here: https://nodecontrol.io/docs/getting-started/updating.html

If anything doesn't work, just reply to this email and I'll sort it out.
